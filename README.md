# Pantelejmon

Pantelejmon is a hands-free, eye-controlled communication aid for people who cannot speak or use their hands but can still move their eyes, including those affected by brain tumors, ALS, stroke, or cerebral palsy.

Using only a laptop webcam, the user looks left, right, up, or down to move a cursor across a large button board and blinks to make a selection. The app then speaks the chosen word or phrase aloud. It includes a grouped letter keyboard with word prediction for free typing, an always available alert button, calibration for each individual user, and it works entirely offline without sending any data off the device.

It was built as an accessible, low cost alternative to clinical eye gaze devices that can cost thousands of euros, so that losing your voice and the use of your hands does not mean losing the ability to be heard.
